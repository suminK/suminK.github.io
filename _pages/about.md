Last updated: Sep 1, 2026.

I am an Assistant Professor at Korea Aerospace University.
My research interests include **stochastic optimization and mixed-integer programming**, motivated by applications to **logistics & transportation systems**. 

I am looking for self-motivated students with a strong interest in optimization theory and its real-world applications. If you are interested in working with me, please feel free to contact me at [suminkang@kau.ac.kr](mailto:suminkang@kau.ac.kr)
